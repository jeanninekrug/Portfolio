About

About

I came to UX from marketing and operations work, where I spent years making processes clearer for clients and colleagues. Now I study how people actually use systems and design to fit them.

I’m a Research Data Management Fellow at the UNC Research Data Management Core, and a master’s student in Information Science at UNC Chapel Hill focused on HCI. I graduate in May 2027.

Before grad school I wrote process handbooks, trained colleagues, standardized client intake across 45+ North Carolina counties, and ran three annual firm conferences.

How I work

  • Interviews and usability sessions are where I do my best work.
  • I put wireframes in front of teammates early and revise from their feedback.

Skills

Research: discovery interviews, synthesis, personas, journey maps, usability testing, accessibility auditing.

Design: sketching, wireframing, prototyping, and visual design in Figma.

Also: cross-functional collaboration and clear writing. Currently learning Python and SQL.
