About

I design from what people actually do.

I’m a graduate student in Information Science at UNC Chapel Hill, focused on human-computer interaction, graduating in May 2027. I’m also a Research Data Management Fellow, where I’m the UX researcher and designer for a self-service portal.

Before graduate school, I worked in legal operations and marketing. I wrote process handbooks, trained colleagues, standardized client intake across dozens of counties, and planned events. That work taught me how confusing a process feels from the outside, which is the starting point for most UX problems.

How I work

  • Start with people. I’m strongest in interviews and usability sessions, where I listen for what people do rather than what they say they do.
  • Say what the evidence can support. My samples have been small, and I label them that way.
  • Design in the open. I wireframe early, put options in front of teammates, and revise.
  • Check accessibility. I audit against WCAG 2.2 AA with automated and manual keyboard testing.

Skills

Research: discovery interviews, thematic synthesis, personas, journey mapping, moderated and unmoderated usability testing, accessibility auditing.
Design: sketching, wireframing, interactive prototyping and visual design in Figma.
Also: cross-functional collaboration and clear written communication. I’m learning Python and SQL, and I don’t write production front-end code.
