03

Canva: where new users get stuck

How can a platform help new users before they get stuck? Our group ran a usability test with four people who had little or no Canva experience, each attempting 10 tasks. Sharing was easy, but most couldn’t make a custom-size poster. I wrote the test script and our findings, and designed a homepage mockup aimed at that problem.

Role
Wrote the test script and findings, ran one session, made the redesign mockup
Context
Course project, team of four, Spring 2026
Methods
Remote usability test, surveys, synthesis, recommendations
